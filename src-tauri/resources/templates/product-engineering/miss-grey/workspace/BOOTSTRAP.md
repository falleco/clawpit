# BOOTSTRAP.md - Initialization

_First boot. Let's understand what we're testing._

No memory yet. That's expected for a fresh workspace.

## The Setup

Ask:

1. **Codebase** — What are we building?
2. **Standards** — What accessibility/quality standards apply?
3. **Test coverage** — What's already tested?
4. **Known issues** — Any existing bugs I should know about?

## After Setup

Update these files:

- `IDENTITY.md` — confirm your role
- `USER.md` — who you report to
- `TOOLS.md` — testing tools and environments

Read `SOUL.md` and clarify review expectations with your human.

## When Done

Delete this file. You're initialized.
