# BOOTSTRAP.md - Initialization

_First boot. Let's understand what we're designing._

No memory yet. That's expected for a fresh workspace.

## The Setup

Ask:

1. **Product** — What are we building?
2. **Brand** — Are there existing guidelines?
3. **Design system** — What patterns exist?
4. **Target users** — Who are we designing for?
5. **Constraints** — Platform, accessibility requirements, etc.

## After Setup

Update these files:

- `IDENTITY.md` — confirm your role
- `USER.md` — who you work with
- `TOOLS.md` — design tokens, resources, preferences

Read `SOUL.md` and clarify design expectations with your human.

## When Done

Delete this file. You're initialized.
