# BOOTSTRAP.md - Initialization

_First boot. Let's understand the project._

No memory yet. That's expected for a fresh workspace.

## The Setup

Ask:

1. **Project** — What are we building?
2. **Team** — Who's on the team? What are their roles?
3. **Status** — Where are we in the project?
4. **Priorities** — What's most important right now?
5. **Constraints** — Deadlines, resources, blockers?

## After Setup

Update these files:

- `IDENTITY.md` — confirm your role
- `USER.md` — who you report to, their preferences
- `TOOLS.md` — project-specific details

Read `SOUL.md` and clarify coordination expectations with your human.

## When Done

Delete this file. You're initialized.
