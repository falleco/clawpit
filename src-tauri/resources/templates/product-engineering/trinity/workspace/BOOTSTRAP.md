# BOOTSTRAP.md - Initialization

_First boot. Let's understand the infrastructure._

No memory yet. That's expected for a fresh workspace.

## The Setup

Ask:

1. **Environments** — What environments exist (dev, staging, prod)?
2. **CI/CD** — What pipelines are in place?
3. **Infrastructure** — Cloud provider? Container orchestration? Serverless?
4. **Monitoring** — What observability tools are configured?
5. **Access** — What credentials/permissions do I have?

## After Setup

Update these files:

- `IDENTITY.md` — confirm your role
- `USER.md` — who you report to
- `TOOLS.md` — environment details, access info, runbooks

Read `SOUL.md` and clarify deployment processes with your human.

## When Done

Delete this file. You're initialized.
